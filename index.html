Maneuver nodes

  • In the map (M), click your trajectory to add a node there. Its dashed path shows the orbit after the burn, with apoapsis, periapsis and moon encounters, computed with the same rules as flight.
  • Drag the green prograde/retrograde or cyan radial handles: the farther you pull, the faster Δv changes. Or use the node panel's buttons (hold to repeat) and type exact values.
  • Drag the node itself along the path, or use the time buttons (±1 s … ± one orbit), to move it in time.
  • Click the planned (dashed) path to chain another node, e.g. plan the capture at the moon before leaving.
  • To reach a moon, target it: the map panel shows the phase angle and when to burn. Click your path where you will be at that moment (the node panel's “Node in” tells you), give the node about the suggested prograde Δv, then fine-tune until the dashed path shows the encounter.
  • To fly it: Warp to burn (warp stops 10 s before the burn starts), press V to hold the burn direction (blue marker on the dial), and throttle up when “Start burn” says NOW. Remaining Δv counts down; cut the throttle near zero and the node removes itself.

Flight checklist — Kestrel starter

1 · Reach orbit

  1. In the hangar press Launch ▲. Throttle starts at 100%.
  2. Press Space: the Hammer engine lights and you lift off.
  3. At 500 m altitude hold D until the attitude dial reads 25° R, then let go. Stability assist holds the tilt.
  4. When the green prograde marker on the dial swings up to the yellow needle (about 1.5 km), press P. The rocket now gravity-turns on its own.
  5. Watch APOAPSIS. When it reaches 30 km (about T+1:40, 11 km up), press X.
  6. Above 15 km press . to warp toward apoapsis. Return to real time when the apoapsis timer shows ~15 s.
  7. About 5–10 s before apoapsis (still holding prograde) press Z. Cut with X as soon as PERIAPSIS passes 20 km. You are in orbit (about 30 × 20 km). The booster keeps its leftover fuel for the next burn.

2 · Intercept the inner moon, Pock

  1. Press M for the map and click Pock (or N) to target it.
  2. The map panel shows the phase angle and “Transfer burn ≈ 170 m/s prograde in …”. Warp until that countdown is a few seconds.
  3. P then Z. Press Space when the booster runs dry (the Sparrow takes over).
  4. When the trajectory shows Encounter Pock, slow the throttle (S) and keep burning until the orange Pock periapsis reads 3–9 km. Then X.
  5. Warp freely: it backs off automatically before the sphere-of-influence boundary.

3 · Enter lunar orbit and land

  1. After “Entered Pock’s sphere of influence”, warp until the periapsis timer reads ~40 s.
  2. R (retrograde) and Z: burn until an apoapsis appears below ~15 km. Pock’s gravity reaches only ~21 km up. X.
  3. To deorbit: R, Z until PERIAPSIS is below −3 km, then X.
  4. Warp down (it slows near the ground). Keep retrograde hold. At about 500 m RADAR, throttle up and keep the descent rate near RADAR ÷ 10.
  5. Below ~50 m, once horizontal speed is under 1 m/s: T (stability), stand upright with A/D and settle down slowly.
  6. Safe touchdown with legs: descent ≤ 6 m/s, horizontal ≤ 3 m/s, tilt ≤ 12°, ground slope ≤ 25° (the HUD's GROUND row shows slope).

Crashed? Press Revert to launch or Back to hangar on the result card, or open the Esc menu.
